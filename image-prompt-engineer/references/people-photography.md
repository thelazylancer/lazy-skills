# People photography

## Build the specification in separate dimensions

Treat these independently:
- Subject identity/age range and only necessary visible attributes
- Body proportions
- Clothing and garment fit
- Pose and hand placement
- Facial expression and gaze
- Camera position and lens-like perspective
- Framing/crop and aspect ratio
- Environment
- Lighting
- Surface texture / photographic finish

## Camera geometry

Always distinguish:
- **Camera position**: eye level, slightly above, low angle, side, over-shoulder, etc.
- **Body orientation**: front-facing, quarter turn, profile, back turned.
- **Gaze**: at camera, off-camera, down, toward an object.
- **Crop**: face, bust, waist-up, three-quarter, full body.

Avoid ambiguous instructions such as "from above" when the intended geometry can be stated explicitly.

## Casual phone-photo realism

For a plausible candid or friend-taken phone photo, favor:
- believable ambient/window/interior light;
- slight but not extreme off-center framing;
- background readable enough to establish place;
- ordinary perspective and depth of field;
- restrained skin variation and small flyaway hairs when appropriate;
- a pose that looks physically motivated rather than studio-perfect.

Do not equate realism with severe noise, blur, tilted horizons, extreme pores, or deliberately poor exposure.

## Selfie logic

Keep capture method coherent:
- Selfie: arm/phone relationship must support the camera viewpoint.
- Mirror selfie: the phone itself is visible in the mirror; its spatial orientation must make sense.
- Third-person/friend-taken: do not introduce selfie arm extension or imply the subject is holding the camera unless requested.

## Example structure

Purpose/style: casual everyday photo taken by a friend on a phone, vertical 3:4.
Subject: one 23-year-old adult woman; use reference identity if supplied.
Clothing/action: plain white T-shirt and blue jeans; seated with both hands naturally on knees.
Environment/composition: beside a home window; camera at eye level; waist-up; slight gaze toward camera.
Light/texture: soft window light; restrained natural skin variation; no heavy beauty retouching; room remains recognizable.
Priority: natural posture and physically plausible everyday lighting.
